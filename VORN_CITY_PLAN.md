# Vorn City transition plan

The existing Godot 4 project is preserved. The first milestone changes the product identity and player flow from Cops & Robbers to Vorn City roleplay without deleting existing assets or legacy systems.

## First milestone

- Rename the project and startup copy to Vorn City.
- Replace the team-selection gate with direct city entry for solo and online flows.
- Remove cops/robbers language from the active login, menu and loading experience.
- Establish a neutral citizen profile and roleplay HUD language.
- Preserve current city streaming, cars, weather, day/night, NPC, GPS, online plumbing and unused legacy assets for later migration.
- Add a V emblem asset for the game icon/branding.

## Next milestones

- Replace match lobby with a city social lobby.
- Add jobs, bank accounts, properties, needs, phone and service interactions.
- Convert the police/weapon gameplay path into lawful city services and emergency systems only where explicitly designed.
- Validate each milestone with Godot headless checks and gameplay capture before moving on.
